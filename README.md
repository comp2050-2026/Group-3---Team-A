# COMP2050 Project 
(Assignment 1 and Assignment 2)

> [!info] Project operating model
> The released 2026 projects are **Project A: ChargeMate Customer** and **Project B: ChargeMate Ops**. The operating model uses Team A/Team B pairing, reciprocal client roles, Week 3 Classroom 50/GitHub setup, Weeks 4–6 client interviews, a Week 7 paired-team review and final clarification activity, Week 7 A1 submission and Week 8 handover/project swap. Problem Analysis is worth 30% and due 13 September 2026; Design Proposal is worth 30% and due 1 November 2026. Both are AI Open and combine group work with individual marks. Released iLearn specifications remain authoritative for assessment rules and later changes.

In this unit you will have two assignments that are linked together. While the assignments have a team submission, it will require everyone to also highlight individual contributions to meeting discussions, brainstorming session, consultation sessions with your assigned client team, and of course, the submission artefacts themselves.

The aim of these assignments is to get you to investigate a problem area while clearly and succinctly describing the problems and also proposing a solution. A lot of the assignment time will involve investigation, discussion and some research rather than coding / writing - in fact, there will be little to no coding.

## Two teams (A and B) make a group

You will form into a team in your week 3 practical class, and there will be another team in the same class that your team is paired up with. That pairing will be called a "group". You will need to decide who will be Team A and who will be Team B within that group.

Your Groups (Group 1, 2, 3, 4) in your practical classes are there to determine what your Team A / Team B pairs are for this assignment. **Please do NOT talk across groups**. Just talk within your own team and with your paired team.

> Team A in your group will develop an SRS for [Project A: ChargeMate Customer](project-briefs/PROJECT_A_CHARGEMATE_CUSTOMER.md).

> Team B in your group will develop an SRS for [Project B: ChargeMate Ops](project-briefs/PROJECT_B_CHARGEMATE_OPS.md).

> Team A will be the "client" / "stakeholder representative" for Project B 
> - Team B will need to keep in regular contact to ask for extra requirements for Project B

> Team B will be the "client" / "stakeholder representative" for Project A
> - Team A will need to keep in regular contact to ask for extra requirements for Project A

**Project A and Project B are separate projects.** The assignment arrangement is described in the Week 1 lecture recording from approximately 1:13 to 1:24. The projects are announced and released in the Week 3 lecture after groups and teams have been formed.

In the Week 3 practical classes, teams set up their GitHub infrastructure through Classroom 50. Every student first accepts the GitHub organization invitation. One member accepts the group assignment, then uses **Manage collaborators** to add the other team members. Selected staff will also have access to team repositories to monitor progress and contributions and help diagnose technical issues. Use the current acceptance link and setup instructions published in iLearn.

## A1 and A2 timeline of activities
The practicals in Weeks 4, 5 and 6 will have time set aside for client interviews and follow-up. All group members need to be present either to act as a client for the paired project or to interview and record the client meeting for their own project. Attendance will be tracked in these classes. In Week 7, paired teams use the restricted Client Review Forum to exchange and review files, ask final clarification questions, agree next steps and upload meeting minutes before finalising and submitting A1.

The weekly breakdown of intended activities for your assignment 1 and 2 are in the timeline link of the assessments section in iLearn.
