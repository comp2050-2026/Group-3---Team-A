# Where is A2?

> [!info] 2026 release and due date
> Design Proposal details are planned for release in Week 7. The 2026 Unit Guide sets an outside release date of 21 September 2026 and a due date of 1 November 2026. iLearn is authoritative for the actual release and submission instructions.

You will receive the details of A2 through iLearn. This includes:
- the list of items to be submitted
- the specification and rubric for each item to be submitted
- the method of submission

Keep an eye out on announcements and lectures for updates.

When those details are released, your group can use the `a2docs` folder in whatever structure works for the group.

See the [A2 Submission and Marking Guide](A2_SUBMISSION_AND_MARKING_GUIDE.md) for the recorded handover, design-document, traceability, testing, sprint, reflection and marking requirements.
