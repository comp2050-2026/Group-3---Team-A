# Project B Brief

**COMP2050 Assignment 1 — Project B, 2026**

## Team B: ChargeMate Ops

*Operator-facing station and inventory system*

ChargeMate Ops: an operator-facing system for running the power bank network at Harbour North Plaza.

## 1. Introduction

You have been approached by ChargeMate, a small company that wants to trial portable phone-charging power banks at Harbour North Plaza.

Harbour North Plaza is a busy Australian shopping centre with a supermarket, food court, cinema, medical centre, library branch, bus interchange, and regular weekend events. ChargeMate plans to place rental stations around the centre so that visitors can borrow a power bank when their phone is running low.

The operations side of the idea is currently called ChargeMate Ops.

## 2. The problem as they see it

ChargeMate can imagine the customer experience, but the staff side is where the idea starts to get messy. Someone has to know whether the stations are working, whether there are enough charged power banks in the right places, and whether any devices need attention.

The shopping centre is not busy in the same way all day. The food court may be busiest at lunch, the cinema in the evening, and the bus interchange during commuter periods. Weekend events may change the pattern again. ChargeMate does not yet know what staff will need to check, record, or act on during a normal day.

The company is also worried about the annoying edge cases that always seem to happen in real services. A power bank may be returned to the wrong place, reported as faulty, left unreturned, or shown differently by different parts of the system. A station might look fine to a customer but still need staff attention.

ChargeMate wants an operator-facing system that helps staff run the pilot without relying on guesswork, sticky notes, or someone remembering to check the same station again later.

## 3. The idea

ChargeMate wants a system that helps staff see what is going on across the power bank network at Harbour North Plaza. The system may help staff check station status, track power banks, respond to faults, move devices between stations, and understand how the service is being used.

The operations system will need to work with the customer-facing system being considered by Team A, but ChargeMate has not yet worked out exactly what information should be shared between the two systems.

ChargeMate is open to useful alerts or recommendations, but this is not an AI project. The main goal is to specify a reliable software system that supports real staff work in a real shopping centre setting.

## 4. Your responsibility

Your task is to plan the software requirements for ChargeMate Ops.

For this assignment, your paired Team A will act as the stakeholder representatives for this project during the scheduled client interview activities. Your requirements should come from this brief and from your interactions with that paired team.

You should focus on the operator-facing system. You should not design the customer rental app, customer payment experience, customer account flow, or kiosk rental journey. Those belong to Team A's project.
