# Project A Brief

**COMP2050 Assignment 1 — Project A, 2026**

## Team A: ChargeMate Customer

*Customer-facing rental system*

ChargeMate Customer: a customer-facing rental experience for portable power banks at Harbour North Plaza.

## 1. Introduction

You have been approached by ChargeMate, a small company that wants to trial portable phone-charging power banks at Harbour North Plaza.

Harbour North Plaza is a busy Australian shopping centre with a supermarket, food court, cinema, medical centre, library branch, bus interchange, and regular weekend events. ChargeMate plans to place rental stations around the centre so that visitors can borrow a power bank when their phone is running low.

The customer-facing part of the idea is currently called ChargeMate Customer.

## 2. The problem as they see it

ChargeMate's first reaction is: this should be simple. A customer finds a station, takes a charged power bank, uses it while they are at the centre, and returns it before they leave.

Once they start thinking about a busy shopping centre, it gets less tidy. Some customers may be in a rush. Some may only want to use the service once. Some may be worried about payment, deposits, late fees, privacy, or what happens if the station does not work as expected.

Customers also need clear information at the right time. They may want to know where the nearest station is, whether there is a charged power bank available, how much the rental will cost, and whether they can return it to a different station.

ChargeMate has also heard a clear message from the shopping centre: customer service staff do not want to spend the day sorting out small technology problems. If something goes wrong, customers should not be left wandering around the centre trying to work out who can help.

## 3. The idea

ChargeMate wants a system that helps shopping centre visitors rent and return portable power banks without making the process feel like a big sign-up exercise. The system might involve a mobile web app, QR codes on the stations, a kiosk screen, SMS messages, or another approach your team thinks is suitable.

The customer system will not work on its own. It will need to exchange some information with the operator-side system being considered by Team B, but ChargeMate has not yet worked out exactly what that should involve.

ChargeMate is open to different ideas, but they want the system to feel like it belongs in a real shopping centre, not like a generic booking app with a power bank label stuck on it.

## 4. Your responsibility

Your task is to plan the software requirements for ChargeMate Customer.

For this assignment, your paired Team B will act as the stakeholder representatives for this project during the scheduled client interview activities. Your requirements should come from this brief and from your interactions with that paired team.

You should focus on the customer-facing rental experience. You should not design the staff operations system, station maintenance process, power bank redistribution process, or operator reporting system. Those belong to Team B's project.
